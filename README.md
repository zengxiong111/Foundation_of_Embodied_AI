# Foundation of Embodied AI

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) | **English**

Chinese and English learning materials for a future Foundation of Embodied AI course, based on our project experience and generated with the assistance of ChatGPT.

The course has two complementary parts—**Part I: Theory & Algorithms** and **Part II: Engineering & Systems**—with **20 topics**. Part I covers foundations, robot learning algorithms and embodied foundation models; Part II covers tools, simulation, policy training, real-time inference and robot-system integration. The parts can be studied in parallel, with no strict sequence.

**Reading paths:** theory research / robotics engineering / full-stack embodied AI. See the [course map](syllabus/COURSE_MAP_EN.md) · <a href="https://zengxiong111.github.io/zengxiong.github.io/files/COURSE_MAP_EN.pdf" target="_blank">PDF</a>.

**Contents:** [Course materials](#course-materials) · [How to learn](#how-to-learn) · [Usage, licensing and feedback](#usage-licensing-and-feedback)

## How to learn

- **Theory research:** deep learning → imitation and generative policies → RL and model-based optimization → VLA/WAM and post-training; supplement vision, dynamics and control as needed.
- **Robotics engineering:** Git/ROS 2 as needed → calibration → dynamics/low-level control → simulation and RL → real-time inference/communication → Robot Agents.
- **Full-stack embodied AI:** study across both parts and connect training to deployment through perception → policy → control → robot → feedback.

See the [course map](syllabus/COURSE_MAP_EN.md) for detailed paths and connections.
